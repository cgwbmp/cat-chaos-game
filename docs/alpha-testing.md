# Cat Chaos alpha: notes for testers

Thank you for trying the game early. It takes about 15 minutes to see enough to help us.

## How to play

Open the link you were sent. It works in a desktop browser and on a phone; on a phone, hold it sideways.

You are a cat. Break things to earn coins. In **Mission** mode you need a coin target before the owner comes home. Stars from missions unlock the next rooms. **Free Soul** mode opens for a room once you have cleared its mission: no timer, break everything.

| Action          | Keyboard             | Phone                 |
| --------------- | -------------------- | --------------------- |
| Move            | WASD or arrow keys   | Drag on the left      |
| Look around     | Mouse                | —                     |
| Jump            | Space (hold: higher) | Jump (hold: higher)   |
| Swat            | J                    | Swat                  |
| Pounce          | K                    | Swat while in the air |
| Zoomies (speed) | Shift                | Zoom                  |
| Pause           | Esc or P             | Pause button          |

## What we would like you to try

1. Play the first room's mission from start to finish without reading anything else.
2. Play at least one more room.
3. Open the shop and the settings once.
4. If you have both, try a phone and a computer.

## What we want to know

- Did you understand what to do in the first minute? If not, where did you get stuck?
- Did the cat feel good to control? Anything slow, slippery or unfair?
- Was the mission too easy, too hard or about right?
- Did the game stutter or run slowly? On which device?
- Would you play another room?

## Reporting a problem

Tell us:

- the version shown at the bottom of the title screen (for example `v0.1.3-alpha`);
- your device and browser (for example "iPhone 13, Safari" or "Windows laptop, Chrome");
- the room and mode you were in;
- what you did, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot or a short recording helps a lot.

Send it to the person who invited you, or open an issue on the game's GitHub page.

## Good to know

- Your progress is saved in your browser only. Clearing site data, or using another browser or device, starts over.
- The backtick key (or a three-finger tap) opens a debug panel. Please do not use its buttons while testing: they add coins and unlock rooms, which hides balance problems.
- Known issues are listed in the changelog. You do not need to report those.
